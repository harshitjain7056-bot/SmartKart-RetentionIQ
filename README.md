# SmartKart RetentionIQ

## 📌 Project Overview

**SmartKart RetentionIQ** is a machine learning-based customer retention solution designed to predict customer churn and identify customers who are at high risk of leaving.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to help SmartKart's retention team:

* Identify customers likely to churn
* Prioritize high-risk customers
* Support proactive retention campaigns
* Make data-driven customer management decisions

## ⚙️ Machine Learning Approach

The project follows an end-to-end ML pipeline covering:

1. Data Collection
2. Data Understanding
3. Data Cleaning
4. Missing Value Treatment
5. Outlier Detection & Treatment
6. Feature Selection
7. Target Definition
8. Train-Test Split
9. Feature Standardization
10. Model Building & Training
11. Churn Prediction
12. Model Evaluation
13. Risk Interpretation
14. Customer-Level Reporting

## 🤖 Model Used

**Logistic Regression** is used as the primary classification model because the objective is to predict a binary outcome—**Churn (1)** or **No Churn (0)**—while also generating churn probabilities.

## 📊 Key Features

* Age
* Monthly Spend
* Complaints

**Customer ID** is excluded from model training because it is an identifier rather than a predictive business feature.

## 📈 Business Output

The solution generates customer churn probabilities and enables customers to be ranked and segmented according to their risk level. This helps retention teams focus resources on customers who need attention most.

## 🚀 Expected Business Impact

SmartKart RetentionIQ converts raw customer data into actionable insights that can help **reduce customer loss, improve retention efforts, and support data-driven decision-making**.

### Tagline

**Predict. Prioritize. Retain.**
